Shipping Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy explains how we ship physical products ordered through the Astronamha store.

1. Delivery area

We deliver across India to all pincodes serviceable by our courier partners. Serviceability is checked at checkout.

2. Dispatch and delivery time

  • Orders are dispatched within 1–3 business days after payment confirmation.
  • Delivery usually takes 3–10 business days depending on your location.
  • Delays may occur due to weather, strikes, festivals or other events beyond our control.

3. Shipping charges

Shipping charges, if any, are shown at checkout before you pay. Any free-shipping offers will be clearly stated in the app.

4. Order tracking

Once your order is shipped, you will receive tracking details by SMS or email, and can follow the status in the app.

5. Incorrect address or failed delivery

Please make sure your address, pincode and phone number are correct. If delivery fails because of an incorrect address or because you were unreachable, the package may return to us; we will contact you to re-ship (re-shipping charges may apply) or refund as per the Refund Policy.

6. Damaged packages

If your package looks tampered with or damaged, please do not accept it, or record an unboxing video and contact us within 48 hours. See our Refund & Cancellation Policy.
