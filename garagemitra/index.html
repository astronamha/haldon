GarageMitra

Order the right spare parts, filters and accessories for your car, bike or commercial vehicle from your phone, delivered across India. Built for vehicle owners, mechanics and garages.

What you get

Find the right part

Search by vehicle make, model and year, or by part name and number, to find parts that fit.

Quality parts & filters

Spare parts, replacement components, accessories and automotive filters, including Haldon International’s own air filters.

For garages & workshops

Bulk ordering and GST invoices for mechanics, workshops and fleet owners.

Delivery across India

Secure online payment, order tracking, and a clear return and refund policy.
