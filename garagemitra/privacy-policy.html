Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Meenakshi Sharma, trading as Haldon International (“Haldon International”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses and protects your personal information when you use the GarageMitra mobile app (Android and iOS) and this website (together, the “Services”). By using the Services you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

We collect only what we need to provide the Services:

  • Account details: name, mobile number and email address, including details from Google or Apple sign-in if you use it.
  • Vehicle details: make, model, year, fuel type and, if you choose to add them, registration number or chassis (VIN) number, used to find parts that fit.
  • Business details (optional): garage or business name and GSTIN, if you want GST invoices.
  • Order details: delivery address, pincode, order history and contact details needed to ship your order.
  • Device and usage data: device model, OS version, app version, crash logs and in-app activity used to improve the app.
  • Location (optional): only if you grant permission, to fill in your delivery address and check delivery to your area.
  • Photos (optional): pictures of parts you upload to find a part or to support a return request.
  • Communications: messages you send to our support team.

2. How we use your information

  • To create your account and show parts that match your vehicle.
  • To process, ship and support your orders, returns and warranty requests.
  • To issue invoices, including GST invoices for business buyers.
  • To send order updates, OTPs and important service messages.
  • To improve, secure and troubleshoot the Services and prevent fraud.
  • To comply with legal obligations such as tax and accounting rules.

3. Payments

Payments are processed by third-party payment gateways. We do not store your card, UPI PIN or net-banking credentials. The gateway shares with us only the payment status and a transaction reference.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers who help us run the Services, and only as needed:

  • Payment gateways, to process payments.
  • Courier and logistics partners, to deliver your orders (name, address, phone number).
  • Part suppliers and manufacturers, only the order details needed to fulfil an order or a warranty claim.
  • Cloud hosting, analytics and crash-reporting providers.
  • Authorities, where required by law or to protect our legal rights.

5. Data retention

We keep your data while your account is active and as long as needed to provide the Services. Order and invoice records are kept for the period required under Indian tax and accounting laws. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal data as described on our Delete Account page, except where we must retain it by law.

6. Your rights and choices

Under applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and withdraw your consent at any time. You can also turn off optional permissions (such as location, camera and notifications) in your device settings. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

7. Children

The Services are not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above shows the latest version. Material changes will be notified in the app or on this website.
