Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy applies to products ordered through the GarageMitra app. If something goes wrong with your order, here is how we put it right.

1. Order cancellation

  • You can cancel an order before it is dispatched from the app or by contacting support. You will receive a full refund.
  • Once an order is dispatched, it cannot be cancelled, but you may refuse delivery or request a return as described below.

2. Returns and replacements

We accept return or replacement requests within 7 days of delivery if:

  • the item is damaged, defective or damaged in transit;
  • you received a wrong or incomplete item;
  • the part does not fit your vehicle, provided it is unused, not installed and in its original sealed packaging (return shipping charges may be deducted if the wrong part was selected by you).

To raise a request, email support@haldoninternational.com or use the app within 48 hours of delivery for damage in transit (7 days for other cases), with your order number and clear photos (or an unboxing video) of the item and packaging.

3. Items that cannot be returned

  • Parts that have been installed, fitted, used or show signs of use.
  • Electrical and electronic parts (such as sensors, ECUs, bulbs and wiring) once the seal or packaging is opened, unless defective on arrival.
  • Oils, lubricants, coolants and other fluids once the seal is broken.
  • Parts specially ordered or sourced for you, unless defective or wrong.

4. Conditions for return

  • The item must be returned with its original packaging, labels, invoice and all accessories.
  • We may inspect the returned item before approving a refund or replacement.

5. Warranty claims

A part that fails after installation is handled under the manufacturer’s warranty, where one is offered. Contact support with your invoice and we will help you with the claim.

6. Refunds

  • Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method. For cash-on-delivery orders, refunds are sent to your bank account or UPI ID.
  • Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days after we approve the request (or receive the returned item); your bank may take additional time to reflect it.
  • If a prepaid order fails or is cancelled by us, the full amount is refunded automatically within 5–7 business days.

7. Purchases through app stores

If any digital item or subscription is purchased through the Apple App Store or Google Play, refunds for it are governed by Apple’s or Google’s refund policies.
