Delete Your GarageMitra Account

Last updated: 24 November 2026

You can ask us to delete your GarageMitra account and the personal data linked to it at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. In the app: open Profile / Settings and choose Delete Account.
  2. By email: write to support@haldoninternational.com from your registered email, or mention your registered mobile number, with the subject “Delete my GarageMitra account”. We may ask you to verify your identity.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile (name, mobile number, email)
  • Saved vehicles and business details
  • Saved addresses, wishlists and in-app preferences

What we may retain

Order, invoice and payment records are retained for the period required by Indian tax and accounting laws, and limited data may be kept to resolve disputes, warranty claims or prevent fraud. This data is not used for any other purpose.

Timeline

Deletion requests are completed within 30 days. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
